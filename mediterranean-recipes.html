Shakshuka with herbs and feta

Prep10 minCook25 minMakes4 people

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely sliced
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • ½ tsp chilli flakes or Aleppo pepper
  • 2 × 400 g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • 6 eggs
  • 60 g feta, crumbled
  • Handful of parsley and coriander, chopped

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a wide frying pan and cook the onion and pepper gently for 10 minutes until soft and sweet.
  2. Add the garlic and spices and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes, season and simmer for 10 minutes until thick. Taste and add the sugar if it’s sharp.
  4. Make six hollows in the sauce, crack in the eggs, cover and cook on low for 6–8 minutes until the whites are set.
  5. Scatter with feta and herbs and serve straight from the pan with warm bread.

Cook’s tip: Cook the eggs with the lid on over low heat so the whites set before the yolks harden.

Lemon and oregano chicken souvlaki

Prep20 minCook12 minMakes4 people

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless chicken thighs, in 3 cm pieces
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Pita, tomatoes, red onion and tzatziki to serve

Method

  1. Mix the oil, lemon zest and juice, garlic, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Add the chicken, toss well, cover and chill for at least 30 minutes or up to 8 hours.
  3. Thread onto skewers (soak wooden ones in water first).
  4. Grill or griddle over high heat for 10–12 minutes, turning often, until charred and cooked through to 74°C (165°F).
  5. Serve in warm pita with tomatoes, onion and a spoonful of tzatziki.

Cook’s tip: Thighs stay juicier than breast on a hot grill and are much more forgiving.

Mujadara: lentils and rice with crispy onions

Prep10 minCook45 minMakes4 people

Ingredients

  • 200 g brown or green lentils, rinsed
  • 150 g long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Plain yogurt and a chopped salad to serve

Method

  1. Simmer the lentils in plenty of water for 15 minutes until just tender, then drain.
  2. Meanwhile fry the onions in the oil over medium heat for 25–30 minutes, stirring often, until deep golden and crisp. Lift out half.
  3. Stir the spices into the remaining onions, add the rice and lentils and stir to coat.
  4. Pour in 450 ml water, season, cover and cook on low for 18 minutes. Rest for 10 minutes off the heat.
  5. Fluff with a fork and top with the reserved crispy onions. Serve with yogurt and salad.

Cook’s tip: Be patient with the onions: their deep caramel flavour is the heart of the dish.

Soft homemade pita bread

Prep20 minCook15 minMakes8 pitas

Ingredients

  • 500 g strong white bread flour
  • 7 g fast-action dried yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300 ml lukewarm water

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt, then add the oil and water and bring together into a soft dough.
  2. Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth, then cover and leave to rise for 1 hour until doubled.
  3. Divide into 8 balls, rest for 10 minutes, then roll each into an oval about 5 mm thick.
  4. Cook one at a time in a very hot dry pan for 1–2 minutes each side until puffed and spotted brown.
  5. Wrap in a clean tea towel as they cook so they stay soft.

Cook’s tip: Keep the pan hot: it’s the burst of steam inside the dough that makes pita puff.

Freekeh salad with pomegranate and herbs

Prep15 minCook25 minMakes4 people

Ingredients

  • 200 g cracked freekeh, rinsed
  • Seeds of 1 pomegranate
  • 1 small cucumber, diced
  • 4 spring onions, sliced
  • Large handful each of parsley and mint, chopped
  • 50 g pistachios, roughly chopped
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp pomegranate molasses
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Simmer the freekeh in salted water for 20–25 minutes until tender with a little bite, then drain and cool.
  2. Whisk the oil, lemon juice and pomegranate molasses with salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the freekeh with the cucumber, spring onions, herbs and most of the pomegranate seeds and pistachios.
  4. Dress, taste for seasoning and finish with the remaining pomegranate and pistachios.

Cook’s tip: Swap freekeh for bulgur or quinoa if you can’t find it; adjust the cooking time.

Pistachio cream tartlets

Prep25 minCook12 minMakes12 tartlets

Ingredients

  • 12 small shop-bought sweet pastry cases, or homemade
  • 100 g shelled unsalted pistachios
  • 2 tbsp icing sugar
  • 250 g mascarpone
  • 100 ml double cream
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp orange blossom water or vanilla
  • Raspberries and chopped pistachios to decorate

Method

  1. If using raw pastry cases, bake them blind according to the recipe or pack, then cool completely.
  2. Blitz the pistachios with the icing sugar until very finely ground.
  3. Whisk the mascarpone, cream, honey and orange blossom water to soft peaks, then fold in the ground pistachios.
  4. Pipe or spoon the cream into the cases.
  5. Decorate with raspberries and chopped pistachios and chill until serving.

Cook’s tip: For the greenest colour, use peeled Sicilian-style pistachios if you can find them.

Bring it together

Serve two or three of these with a few dips and you have a feast. Use the mezze planner to work out quantities, mix fresh spice blends for extra flavour, and check what’s in season before you shop.
