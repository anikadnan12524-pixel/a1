About Pistachio Glen

A home-cooking guide for anyone who loves the generous, sunny food of the Mediterranean and the Middle East.

Our story

Why Pistachio Glen?

Pistachio Glen grew out of long weekend meals: a table crowded with small plates, bread torn by hand and conversations that ran on long after the food was gone. We wanted to share that way of cooking with home cooks everywhere, using ingredients you can find in an ordinary supermarket.

We focus on seasonal Mediterranean and Middle Eastern home cooking: mezze, salads, grills, grains and simple sweets. Alongside our recipes you’ll find practical tools to plan a spread, mix spice blends and shop with the seasons. Every recipe is cooked in a home kitchen and adjusted before it’s published.

Pistachio Glen is independent. We don’t sell food or kitchen equipment and we aren’t paid to recommend brands. The cuisines we cook from belong to many countries and communities, and we try to describe their dishes with care and respect.
